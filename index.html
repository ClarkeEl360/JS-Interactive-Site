Who We Are

The Rose Gold Pirates are a pirate group that puts most of its value into its crew. Unlike other pirates, we do not use the same class system; most of our voting powers go into the crew, with the captain only getting 10% of the voting power. We also hold bi-monthly votes on who the captain should be; though this vote decides the captain, it doesn’t take away from our old tradition of dueling for the title of captain, so don’t worry if you’re used to this/like this tradition.
